A message from our leadership

Welcome to the Room2Bloom family.

Dear Parents,

It is with great pleasure that we welcome you and your child to Room 2 Bloom Community Development Center. We are honored that you have become a part of our family.

Our goal is to impact our students’ lives educationally, emotionally and socially so they have a strong foundation for their educational career. We strive to provide not only a challenging academic program but also a secure, personal environment of care for each individual student.

Our team looks forward to working with you. Welcome once again.

Daycare program

Care around the clock.
We call it Care24/7.

The center operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so every family can find care that fits its schedule, whether that's early mornings, overnight shifts or weekends. Our curriculum follows the National Educational School Readiness goals to set every child on the right track for success.

Ages 8 weeks – 1 year

Infant Nursery

Our Infant Care program follows learn from the start, a milestone-based curriculum that nurtures your baby's growth from the very first weeks.

Ages 1 – 5 years

Toddler & Preschool

Math and literacy learning plus plenty of chances for social development, with teachers who believe in every child.

Every day

Healthy Meals

Breakfast, lunch and snacks are provided, so children are fueled to play, explore and learn all day long.

The vision

A fresh approach to early learning.

Children are active, participatory learners. Every activity is designed to develop each child intellectually, physically and emotionally. The center will be a home away from home for infants, toddlers, children and pre-adolescents: a safe haven with emotional support that promotes excellence academically and socially.

We're revitalizing our facility into a light, airy, modern space with eco-friendly furnishings made from organic cotton, wool, recycled fabrics and reclaimed wood, and with organically defined stations for children to play and learn.
